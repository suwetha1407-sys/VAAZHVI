ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Introducing VAAZHVI

VAAZHVI is an AI-Based Personal Health Guidance Assistant designed to help individuals understand their health-related information and build better everyday wellness habits. It brings personal profiles, health history, reminders and lifestyle information into a single platform.
